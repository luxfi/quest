How it works

  1. 01Pin the placesDrop stops on a map. Give each one a clue and a point value.
  2. 02Name a prizeWhatever you want to offer, in your own words.
  3. 03Send the linkThe whole quest travels in the link. No accounts, nothing to install.
  4. 04They walk itEach stop unlocks only when they actually get there.

Prizes are between you and whoever you send it to — Lux Quest doesn’t hold money or hand anything out. The quest lives in the link itself, so nobody needs an account and no copy is kept.
